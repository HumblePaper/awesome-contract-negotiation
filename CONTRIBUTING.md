# Contributing

Thank you for suggesting an entry. These rules apply to every list in this family.

## The rules

- One entry per pull request.
- One line per entry, in the format `- [Name](url) - One sentence ending in a full stop.`
- Keep each section in alphabetical order by name.
- The link goes to the thing itself (the form, the dataset, the tool, the community), not to a page about it.
- Write plain English. No marketing adjectives, and no em dashes.
- Do not add rankings or claims you cannot point to on the linked page.
- Run `npx awesome-lint` before you open the pull request. The same check runs in CI, along with a link check.

## What belongs here

An entry belongs if all three are true:

- A negotiator can use it: someone who negotiates commercial contracts (in-house counsel, contracts managers, deal counsel, founders, finance leads) can pick it up and use it in a live deal.
- It is maintained, or still correct. A form that has not changed in years is fine if the law and the market have not moved.
- The link is the thing itself: the form, the dataset, the tool, the community.
- One line per product. A company with two products gets two lines.
- For a tool, the linked page says what it does for negotiation, review or redlining.
- The funding and hiring tails on tool entries are optional. Each must link a source that states it.
- No vendor claims about customers or speed.
- A product that is acquired, renamed or closed moves to the Acquired, merged and closed section with a linked source, rather than being deleted.

Legal-tech directories, legal NLP reading lists and general legal research tools belong on the related lists linked from the README.
