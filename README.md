## Model forms and standards

- [Bonterms](https://bonterms.com/download-center/) - Free standard NDAs, Cloud Terms, PSAs and DPAs, negotiated through a cover page instead of redlines.
- [Common Paper](https://commonpaper.com/standards/) - Free standard agreements, maintained by a committee of attorneys under CC BY 4.0.
- [GOV.UK Model Services Contract](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/model-services-contract) - The UK government's template for high value, complex services.
- [ISDA documentation](https://www.isda.org/books/) - The library of ISDA master agreements, definitions and user guides for derivatives.
- [LMA documents](https://www.lma.eu.com/documents-guidelines) - Loan market documentation, searchable by sector and market segment.
- [Manual of Style for Contract Drafting](https://www.adamsdrafting.com/writing/mscd/) - Ken Adams's reference for how contract language should read.
- [NVCA model legal documents](https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/) - Free venture financing templates, meant as starting points to adapt to each deal.
- [oneNDA](https://www.onenda.org/) - A crowd-sourced, open-source NDA that is free to use.
- [Y Combinator SAFE](https://www.ycombinator.com/safe) - The free, standardized agreement startups use to raise early money.

## Playbooks and position guides

- [Fact gathering in software negotiations](https://project.linuxfoundation.org/hubfs/Reports/Fact-Gathering-Software-Negotiations-Whitepaper_043020.pdf) - A Linux Foundation whitepaper on establishing the facts about third-party and open source components before negotiating a software deal.
- [GC AI contract playbook template](https://gc.ai/blog/contract-playbook-template) - A copy-and-paste playbook template with six clause examples showing preferred, fallback and walk-away positions.
- [Model Services Contract Guidance](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/68af2474960e2d135b4c8eb2/Buyer_Guidance_-_MSC_v2.2A_2025.pdf) - The UK government's buyer guidance to its model services contract, with key negotiation issues on liability, indemnity, insurance and due diligence.
- [Public Cloud Service Agreements: What to Expect and What to Negotiate](https://www.omg.org/cloud/deliverables/CSCC-Public-Cloud-Service-Agreements-What-to-Expect-and-What-to-Negotiate.pdf) - The Cloud Standards Customer Council's ten-step guide to evaluating and negotiating cloud service agreements.

## Precedent and clause sources

- [ASIC registers](https://www.asic.gov.au/online-services/search-asic-registers) - Search Australian company and regulated-entity registers.
- [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk) - UK company filings, charges and accounts.
- [EDGAR full-text search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) - Search the text of filings at the SEC, including contracts filed as exhibits.
- [Law Insider](https://www.lawinsider.com) - A public contract database and clause search, with a free tier.
- [Onecle](https://www.onecle.com) - Business contracts from SEC filings, browsable by contract type, industry and company name.

## Datasets and benchmarks

- [ContractNLI](https://stanfordnlp.github.io/contract-nli/) - Document-level natural language inference over NDAs.
- [CUAD](https://www.atticusprojectai.org/cuad/) - 510 commercial contracts with 13,000+ labels across 41 clause types, from the Atticus Project.
- [LEDGAR](https://github.com/dtuggener/LEDGAR_provision_classification) - Provision classification over SEC contracts.
- [LexGLUE](https://github.com/coastalcph/lex-glue) - A benchmark of legal language understanding tasks in English.
- [MAUD](https://github.com/The-Atticus-Project/maud) - The Merger Agreement Understanding Dataset, curated by the Atticus Project.

## Negotiation agents

Tools that take the counterparty's draft and return redlines or run rounds with little human input.

- [Cyberbase](https://www.cyberbase.ai) - Applies a company's approved playbook to incoming contracts and proposes tracked changes in DOCX files for the team to review. It checks the full document, flags sections that differ from the business's position, and also handles security questionnaires in Excel and PDF. It runs single-tenant inside the customer's own Azure or AWS subscription and is used by legal, compliance and security teams on NDAs, data processing addenda and master services agreements. The legal entity is Cyberbase Systems, Inc. [hiring on Workable: no open roles](https://apply.workable.com/cyberbase/).
- [Flank](https://flank.ai) - Agents that handle redlining, negotiation rounds and review of contracts by email or browser, supervised by legal. They draft, review, negotiate and triage against a customer's templates, terms, playbooks and escalation rules, and lawyers review finished work and high-risk items. Business teams send requests by Outlook email or an intranet portal, and Flank connects to Teams, Slack, CLMs, Jira and Salesforce. It is for enterprise legal teams and law firms and covers NDAs, DPAs, service agreements, statements of work and vendor agreements. [US$10M round, 5 Jun 2025](https://insightpartners.com/ideas/flank-raises-10m-to-scale-autonomous-legal-agents-embedded-invisible-and-built-for-the-enterprise); [careers page: no open roles](https://flank.ai/careers).
- [Leela Works](https://leela.works) - An email-native contract negotiation agent that builds and evolves its own playbooks through a learning loop, connecting rules, positions and precedents into a negotiation intelligence graph. Leela returns issues lists, tracked redlines and negotiation memos in minutes. Built for in-house legal teams, law firms and deal teams handling complex commercial agreements and multi-document transactions.
- [Negotiagent](https://negotiagent.ai) - Trains an AI agent on a company's past deals and recommends redlines in each negotiation round through a Word plug-in. It learns from the company's full negotiation history and steers redlines toward what it calls an Acceptable Contract Range. The site says it is built to negotiate contracts on a user's behalf rather than to suggest drafting edits, and that it does not rely on playbooks. The company is Negotiagent B.V.
